Q2

Financial instruments

Usually 10 marks: compound instruments, derivatives or effective interest

Individual Ind AS

4 to 8 mark questions, one standard each. Revenue, employee benefits, PPE and leases come most

Theory

Framework and technology

Short 4 to 5 mark answers on the framework, ethics and accounting technology

SAFEST 24 MARKS

Master Q1 and Q2 first

Consolidation and financial instruments came in every one of the six exams, in the same slots.

EVERY EXAM

Ind AS 115 Revenue

Small, about 5 to 6 marks, but it has not missed a single exam.

DO NOT SKIP

The small standards

Marks are spread over twenty-plus standards, so leaving several of them can cost a full question.
