When price rises and the quantity demanded stays the same, demand is

A. Unit elastic
B. Perfectly inelastic
C. Perfectly elastic
D. Relatively elastic
Correct. Quantity does not respond to price at all, so elasticity is zero and the demand curve is vertical.
