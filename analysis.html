What to study first

A

Study first

63% of the marks
  • Company Accounts
  • Partnership and LLP
  • Accounting Process
  • Not-for-Profit Organisations (NPO)

All four came in every one of the 8 exams. Practise full long questions with a timer.

B

Study next

26% of the marks
  • Final Accounts of Sole Proprietors
  • Incomplete Records
  • Depreciation
  • Theoretical Framework

About 8 marks each on average, often as a full 10-mark question.

C

Learn the basics

11% of the marks
  • Bank Reconciliation (BRS)
  • Inventories
  • Bills of Exchange

Mostly 5-mark either/or questions now. Learn the formats.

What each question usually asks

Q1

Must attempt · 20 marks

6 true or false with reasons (12 marks), one theory answer (4), one journal or purchase/sales book (4)

Q2

Depreciation and errors

A machinery account with depreciation plus correcting wrong entries. This pair came in 5 of 8 exams

Q3

A mix of two chapters

Two from: partnership, final accounts, incomplete records, or accounts of a club (NPO)

Q4

Partnership, every time

A partner joins, leaves, dies or the firm closes. Plus final accounts, NPO or incomplete records

Q5

Small questions + company

BRS, inventory or bills as 5-mark either/or, then a 10-mark company question

Q6

Company accounts

A 15-mark question on issue and forfeiture of shares or redeeming preference shares, plus 5 marks of theory

Q1 is a must. Then choose any 4 from Q2 to Q6. Every question is 20 marks.

GROWING

Depreciation: 8 to 10 marks in each of the last 4 exams

Incomplete Records also gave 10 marks or more in 4 of the last 5 exams.

SHRINKING

BRS and Bills of Exchange

No full BRS question since May 2025. Bills gave no compulsory marks in the last two exams.

REMEMBER

Don't leave any chapter fully

You choose 4 out of 5 questions. Leaving two chapters can cost you a 20-mark question.

What to study first

A

Study first

78% of the marks
  • Indian Contract Act
  • Sale of Goods Act
  • Companies Act
  • Indian Partnership Act

20 marks or more each, in every exam. Practise writing case-study answers.

B

Study next

12% of the marks
  • Negotiable Instruments Act

Exactly 14 marks in all 8 exams, as two 7-mark questions.

C

Learn the basics

10% of the marks
  • Limited Liability Partnership Act
  • Indian Regulatory Framework

One 6-mark question each, in every exam. Short chapters, easy marks.

The same pattern, exam after exam

Q1

Must attempt · 20 marks

Sale of Goods + Companies

Partnership + Companies

Q6

Bills + Contract + Sale

This layout held in nearly every one of the 8 exams. Q1 is a must, then choose any 4 from Q2 to Q6.

HOW TO WRITE A CASE ANSWER

Issue, then the rule, then your conclusion

Say what the question is about, state the law, then apply it to the facts.
